Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions

How long does a typical repair take?

Most repairs are completed within 24-48 hours. Simple repairs like screen replacements can often be done while you wait (2-4 hours). Complex repairs may take longer depending on part availability.

Do you offer free diagnostics?

Yes! We offer free diagnostics for all devices. We'll identify the issue and provide you with a detailed quote before any repair work begins.

What types of payment do you accept?

We accept cash, all major credit cards (Visa, MasterCard, American Express), and digital payments (Apple Pay, Google Pay).

Is there a warranty on repairs?

Yes! All our repairs come with a 90-day warranty on both parts and labor. If you experience any issues related to the repair, we'll fix it for free.

Do I need to schedule an appointment?

Walk-ins are welcome! However, we recommend scheduling an appointment for faster service, especially for complex repairs. You can schedule online or call/WhatsApp us at +254 723 754 676.
