About JeffTech

Your trusted partner for electronics repair and quality products since 2020

Our Story

JeffTech Electronics was founded in 2020 with a simple mission: to provide honest, reliable, and affordable electronics repair services to our community.

What started as a small repair shop has grown into a full-service electronics destination, serving thousands of satisfied customers every year. We believe that everyone deserves access to quality electronics repair without the stress of complicated pricing or confusing technical jargon.

Our founder, Jeff Maina, combined his 15 years of experience in electronics engineering with a passion for helping people to create a repair experience that puts customers first. Today, our team of certified technicians continues that tradition of excellence.
